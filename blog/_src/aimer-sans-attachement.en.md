# Love Without Attachment: How to Love Freely Without Fear of Loss

What does it mean to love without attachment? In life, we often fall in love with people and things that bring us happiness, fulfillment and a sense of purpose. This love can be an extraordinary experience. But as time passes, we may begin to cling to these people or things, the way a jellyfish holds on to whatever it has caught. Little by little, we come to believe that life would be unbearable without them, and anxiety begins to appear. This attachment can cloud our minds and dim the joy and connection we once felt.

### How Attachment Creates Anxiety

The ego becomes anxious and fearful when it forms attachments, especially to people. We may worry about not getting a call, about not spending enough time together, or about having different love languages. The ego also tends to imagine scenarios in which we lose what we hold dear, so we end up worrying about things that are not even real.

It is similar when we are attached to objects or to money: we may feel anxious about losing them or giving them away. Our initial happiness begins to fade as we move into a defensive mode, holding on tightly to what we are attached to so that we don't lose it. This fear-driven way of living makes us suffer, and much of that suffering comes simply from the possibility of losing what we love.

### You Are the Source of What You Feel

It helps to remember that we cannot receive anything we don't already carry in some way. To receive something, it must already exist inside of us. We are the ones generating our emotions, both the beautiful ones and the difficult ones, and the power lies within us, not in the people or things we attach ourselves to. When we form attachments, we give our power away, believing that we cannot exist on our own and that we need something outside of us to make our lives complete.

These people and things may reflect the joy, beauty and love that we already have, but they are not the source of these feelings. They are reminders of who we really are. Letting go of attachment does not mean abandoning our relationships or pulling away from what we love. It means recognizing our own power and understanding that we are the ones generating what we feel.

### Loving Like We Watch a Sunset

When you see the other person as complete, like a mirror, you also recognize yourself as complete, and then you can come together from your wholeness. One way to practice loving without attachment is to enjoy our experiences the way we enjoy a sunset. We can watch it, live it and fully appreciate its beauty. Then, when the time comes, we can let it go and still feel happy because we were able to experience such beauty. This way of loving allows us to love more freely and more authentically.

> Loving without attachment is a purer form of love, one that frees us from neediness, fear and insecurity, and allows us to truly love others, and ourselves.

Love without attachment is a practice, and it can transform our lives, our relationships and our experiences. As we recognize our own power and understand that we are the true source of our emotions, we can love more freely and more truthfully. This kind of love brings a sense of freedom that deepens our connection with ourselves and with others, and enriches our lives in ways we cannot yet imagine.
