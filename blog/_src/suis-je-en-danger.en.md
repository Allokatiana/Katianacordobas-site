# Am I in Danger? How to Feel Safe Being Your Authentic Self

Am I in danger? How can I feel safe? So much of our inner life comes down to one simple and profound truth: human beings need to feel safe. From the moment we come into this world, our most basic instinct is survival. As newborns, we rely on our parents to create a sense of safety, a cocoon where we feel secure, loved and protected. This primal need is the foundation on which our emotional and psychological well-being is built.

But what happens when that sense of safety is threatened? When, as children, we are yelled at, mistreated, or asked to hide who we truly are in order to meet other people's expectations? Little by little, we begin to disconnect from our authentic selves.

### The Cost of Disconnecting From Ourselves

As young children, we quickly learn that expressing what we truly want, or simply being who we are, might lead to rejection, punishment or ridicule. To protect ourselves, we adapt. We become what others, our parents, teachers, peers and society, want us to be, and this is how many of us lose touch with our inner selves.

Over time, this adaptation becomes second nature. We wear masks, versions of ourselves carefully shaped to gain approval and avoid conflict. But these masks come at a cost: we lose our authenticity. Eventually, the fear of being who we are pushes us to conform so completely that we no longer remember what was underneath.

### The Cycle of Inauthenticity

When we grow up wearing these masks, we often pass them on to the next generation. As parents, if we have not done the inner work to heal and reconnect with our authentic selves, we can unconsciously teach our children to do the same. Without realizing it, we may impose our ideas and expectations on them and repeat the cycle of conditional love: "Behave well," "Do as I say," "Be who I want you to be." The result is another generation disconnected from its inner truth, living in fear of rejection and shaped more by outside influences than by its own desires and purpose.

### Reconnecting With Ourselves Through Inner Work

Breaking this cycle begins with us. Inner work helps us reconnect with who we truly are and gently shed the layers of conditioning that kept us from being authentic. As the masks fall away, we can rediscover what we genuinely want and need. As we align with our authentic selves, we naturally move toward our true purpose. And by living authentically, we give our children permission to embrace their own unique paths.

### Parenting With Authenticity

When we have done this inner work, we can raise our children in a way that nurtures their inner strength and individuality. We can respect their path, understanding that their journey belongs to them and not to us. We can create a safe space where they can express themselves without fear of rejection. And we can guide them with love and patience, teaching with compassion, understanding and openness rather than with control or criticism.

When children grow up feeling safe to be themselves, they don't need masks. They learn to move through the world with confidence and authenticity, less shaken by outside pressures. They can live in their own flow, fulfill their own mission and contribute to the world in a meaningful way.

### A New Way Forward

> The journey back to authenticity begins with self-acceptance.

When we embrace who we truly are, we create a ripple effect that touches our children, our relationships and, ultimately, the world. Let us break the cycle of inauthenticity and create a future where being ourselves feels safe, celebrated and fulfilling.
